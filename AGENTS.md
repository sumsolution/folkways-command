# folkways command

Brain repo for building [folkways](https://github.com/sumsolution/folkways) with folkways. It holds no
code: sessions start here, and changes to the framework happen in worktrees of `folkways` under
`projects_dir`.

## Way of working

The framework's rules, kept current by `folkways sync`. Don't remove this line:

@.folkways/instructions.md

## Team notes

- Framework rules for the `folkways` repo itself are in its `AGENTS.md`; follow them in its worktrees.
- Design records, plans and research live here (`knowledge/`, `efforts/`), not in the `folkways` repo.
